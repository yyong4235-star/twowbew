BMS smart DJ

Privacy Policy

Applies to the DJ ELECTRONICS website and the Google Play version of the BMS smart DJ mobile app.

Last Updated: August 2, 2026 | Effective Date: August 2, 2026

1. Core Principle

BMS smart DJ is a professional local debugging tool for BMS battery management boards. The app is designed for local operation, local Bluetooth connection, and local storage. It does not require account registration, does not actively collect personally identifiable information, does not automatically upload user data to servers, and is not used for advertising, user tracking, cross-app tracking, or user profiling.

2. Personal Information Collection

The app does not actively collect, store, or upload the user's name, phone number, email address, contacts, SMS, call logs, photos, camera content, account information, advertising identifiers, precise location, or background location data. Tracking, privacy collection, and data sharing items reviewed by app platforms are not functional purposes of this app.

3. Data Processed Locally

To search, pair, connect, display data, and configure parameters for BMS devices, the app may process BMS battery management board operating data locally on the user's device, such as battery voltage, current, temperature, balancing status, protection thresholds, fault status, connection records, configuration data actively saved by the user, and battery historical operating records saved locally. This data is stored only on the user's device and is not automatically synchronized or uploaded to any server. Unless the user actively exports, screenshots, copies, or sends such data to us for technical support, we do not access this local data.

4. Permission Usage

BLUETOOTH, BLUETOOTH_SCAN, and BLUETOOTH_CONNECT permissions are used only to search, pair, and connect local BMS battery management boards, and to send and receive operating data such as battery voltage, temperature, and balancing status with the device. ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION is used only to support the mandatory BLE scanning requirement on Android 11 and earlier. The app does not read, record, use, store, or upload the user's actual geographic location. INTERNET permission is not used for account login, advertising, analytics, cloud synchronization, or user data upload. Storage read/write permissions are used only to save battery historical operating records and user-selected local files on the device, and are not used for external synchronization.

5. Purpose of Data Use

Local data is used only to search and connect BMS battery management boards, display real-time battery and BMS status, read or configure BMS parameters, save local configurations and historical records selected by the user, improve Bluetooth communication stability, and support local debugging. The app does not use this data for advertising, commercial analytics, data sales, third-party sharing, or purposes unrelated to BMS management.

6. Storage, Deletion, and Security

Operating data, configuration data, historical records, and logs are stored locally on the user's device by default and are protected by the operating system sandbox. Users can clear local data in the app or through system settings. When the app is uninstalled, the operating system usually removes app cache and local configurations. If the user actively exports or shares local files, those files are managed by the user.

7. Support Materials Voluntarily Provided by Users

If a user voluntarily contacts us by email, phone, or other means and provides screenshots, logs, device data, issue descriptions, or contact information, we will use such materials only for technical support, troubleshooting, and after-sales communication. We will not use them for advertising, profiling, sale, or purposes unrelated to support. Users may request deletion of support materials they voluntarily provided by contacting us at the email address in this policy.

8. Third-Party SDKs and Data Sharing

The current version does not integrate any third-party SDK, including advertising, analytics, tracking, payment, map, cloud storage, or user analysis SDKs. We do not sell, rent, or share users' personal information or local BMS device data with third parties. If a future version integrates third-party services or changes data processing practices, we will update this policy before use and obtain necessary authorization according to platform rules.

9. Children and Minors

The app is a professional tool for BMS battery management board development, debugging, maintenance, and industrial use. It is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information. Minors should not use the app independently; if use is necessary, it should be supervised by a guardian or professional.

10. User Rights

Users may view, modify, export, or delete available local configuration data and historical records in the app, or stop using and uninstall the app. Because the app does not require account registration and does not automatically upload user data, we usually cannot identify, access, or delete local data stored on the user's device from a server side. For support materials voluntarily sent to us, users may contact us by email to request access or deletion.

11. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy due to app function changes, platform policy changes, or legal requirements. The updated version will be published on the company website or the public privacy policy page used for app store listings. Continued use of the app means you understand the updated content.

12. Governing Law

Because the app does not automatically collect or upload users' personal data by default, it generally does not involve server-side user account data processing or cross-border transfer. If a user voluntarily provides technical support materials to us, we will use them only within the scope necessary to handle the issue.
